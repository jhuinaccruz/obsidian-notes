# Chapter 1
## Section 1
>*All Illinois residents are required to have the following types of licenses and or permits in order to legally be permitted to drive in the State of Illinois:*
>- *Valid Illinois driver’s license,*
>- *Valid temporary visitor driver license*
>- *Valid probationary license*
>- *Valid Instruction Permit*
>- *Valid Restricted Driving Permit*
>- *Valid Monitoring Device Driving Permit*


>*How to obtain an Illinois Driver’s License:*
>- *Driver license applicants must be at least 18 years old or older.*
>- *Go to any Illinois Driver Services Facility to show required identification documents and materials. You will have a new ID photo taken at this time.*
>- *Give up; surrender all out-of-state licenses (if any). Including any commercial driver’s licenses, out-of-state Identification (ID) cards and or any instruction permits.*
>- *Pay the Driver Service Facility the required licensing fee*
>- *Pass all required driver exams such as vision screening, written driver test and or a possible actual driving, behind-the-wheel exam.*
>- *Provide Driver Services Facility with a valid, Illinois mailing address for the Illinois state issued permanent driver’s license.*
