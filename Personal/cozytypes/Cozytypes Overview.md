>[!abstract]
>*Cozytypes is a minimalist typing test website designed for keyboard enthusiasts to practice and improve their typing speed and accuracy. It offers customizable features, including themes, fonts, and typing difficulties. Users can take tests of various durations (15, 30, 60, or 120 seconds) and word sets (10, 25, 50, or 100 words). The platform also tracks stats like WPM (words per minute) and accuracy and provides leaderboards for those with accounts. Signing up allows users to save their progress and compete with others.*
