*Ideally, a note object is made up of its properties, content, and links, where said object can be seen as a sum of all three or through the lens of either characteristic(s)*

## Properties
*Properties are denoted at the top of the note (as YAML)*

*Every note should have these seven properties*:
- __Created__: Date created
- __Kind__: What *kind* of note is it?
	- *Reference*: Concept f(or indexing and later querying) of a function/specific piece of knowledge
	- *Lecture*: Notes from a lecture
	- *Analysis*: Analysis of anything personal
	- *Plan*: A guide/structuring of a process/project/etc.
	- *Study*: Scholarly papers, scientific papers, any formal source with a DOI
	- *Miscellaneous*: Anything else (needs categorization ASAP)
- __Domain__: What broad category of study does this belong to?
	- e.g. Psychology, Data Science/Coding, Music, etc.
	- __Subdomain__: What topic within the larger domain?
- __Last Updated__: Last time the notes were updated
- __State__: How confident and well-read do I feel about what I am writing down?
	- Use Likert Scale (1-7)
- __Action__: What do i need to do next regarding this topic?
	- *Archive*: Reviewed and used, in other words nothing (mostly for school/study) (==Default==)
	- *Study*
	- *Revise*: Unfinished note needs to be finished

*Some categories more specific to certain notes can include things like*:
- __Project__: Is this knowledge contributing to a specific product or goal? If so what?
	- __Subproject__: A project within a project; a large enoguh component of a project to deserve its own section
