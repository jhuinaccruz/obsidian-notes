# Group Notes

__Summary__:
One organized creative network where people can showcase their work, discover artists and communities, connect with fans and collaborators, and find events or opportunities without jumping between a dozen platforms.
Long-term ecosystem: music, fashion, film, photography, visual art, festivals, venues, magazines, and creative organizations.

__Problem__:
Creative communities are fragmented. Artists, musicians, designers, filmmakers, photographers, fans, and industry organizations must jump between Instagram, LinkedIn, Discord, Eventbrite, portfolio sites, newsletters, and company pages to find people, events, and opportunities. This wastes time, creates information overload, and makes creative industries feel harder to access.

__Goal__:
- Create one organized, welcoming space where creatives can showcase their work, discover people and opportunities, build authentic communities, and strengthen artist-to-fan relationships.
- Hackathon goal: prove the concept with one focused, working user journey instead of attempting the full social network.

__Audience(s)__:
- Emerging musicians, artists, photographers, filmmakers, fashion designers, and other creatives who need visibility, collaborators, events, and opportunities.
- Secondary: fans, festivals, venues, galleries, magazines, labels, recruiters, and creative organizations looking to discover and support talent.

__Design Principles__:
- Creative
- Welcoming
- Organized
- Community-first
- Culturally curious
- Expressive
- Accessible
- Credible
The experience should feel exciting and human—not corporate, cluttered, elitist, or gatekept.
