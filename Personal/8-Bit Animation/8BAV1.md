## Notes:
__Animation__: *sequencing of imagery to produce the illusion of movement*

>[!question] *How to animate?*
>*Add frames to the artwork, displaying them back to back*

### Timing
>*Standard television is around 24 fps...the most common fps for 8-bit animation is 12*

>S(peed) = distance / time

### Planning
>[!important]
>Planning movements is important to make good animations

>*Animation isn't about lighting or the character, its about the __movement__*

>[!important]
>Don't edit pixels, edit motion

### Movement
>[!warning ]
>*A common mistake is to depict every single frame*
 
>*To make your movement nice and crunchy, don't worry about drawing so many frames. Just do a couple.*

This can make things look choppy, so use:
- __Smearing__
>*Smearing acts like motion blur does in real footage by stretching the object into a blotch of color that signifies direction. This shape tells us both the speed and the direction the subject is moving*
- __Anticipation__
	Some anticipation frames can add a nice touch to drastic movements
- __Secondary Animations__
>*Secondary animation is where things in your world react to the primary movement*
- __Easing in/out__
>*To ease in and out, exponentially decrease the distance your sprite moves for the start and end of its movement*

More techniques:
- __Sub-pixel Animation__
	Useful for smoothing out animations
	1. *Add more frames to work with*
	2. *Smear individual pixel movements*
		smear and smooth individual pixels (the anti-aliasing of pixel animation) 
	1. *Edit the outline*
- __Overshooting__
	When the subject goes too far in their range of motion
>*Always go for bigger, over-exaggerated movements*
