__Linear Model__: A straight line through the data, modeled as $$y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1x_i + e_i$$
- where (for every value $x_i$) there is
	- an intercept ($B_0$)
	- a scalar ($B_i$)
	- an error $e_i$
		- The error is distributed as a normal distribution, that is to say $e_i \sim N(0, \sigma^2)$
