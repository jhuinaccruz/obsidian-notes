__Grammar of Graphics__: Methodology of data graphics such that they convey specific information efficiently
 - In R: `ggplot2`

__Coordinate Systems__:
- Cartesian: Rectangular coordinates (`x, y`)
- Polar: Similar to Cartesian; coordinates denoted by their radius and angle as (𝜌, 𝜃)
- Geographic: Represents locations on the curved surface of the Earth

__Scaling Systems__:
- Numeric
	- Linear
	- Logarithmic
	- Percentile
- Categorical: Based on naming schemes
- Time: Categorical, numerical, periodic, etc.

>*The purpose of data graphics is to help the viewer make meaningful comparisons*
