>- Lesbian and bisexual women are more likely than heterosexual women to report childhood abuse and adult sexual assault
>- __Population__: *...adults sexual minority women living in the US (N = 1,243)*
>- __Variables__: *...examined differenes in both childhood abuse and adult sexual assault by women's current gender identity*

## Introduction

>*Compared to men, women are far more likely to be victims of abuse

>*In our review of the literature, all studies are based in the United States unless otherwise stated and include both heterosexual sexual minority samples as well as both adult and child samples*

The data was collected by the Rainbow Women's Project, a "*national, web-based survey of adult women who identify as lesbian/gay and bisexual*"

In the study, a nominal (categorical) measure was used with the self-reported gender identities (butch, femme, androgynous, other). Additionally, a continuous measure was used for one's gender expression (from more butch/masculine to more femme/feminine)

### Sexual Orientation and Trauma

>*Among women, sexual minorities appear to be at heightened risk for experiencing trauma across their lives*

>*...sexual minority women are more likely to report histories of childhood emotional, physical, and sexual abuse compared to heterosexual women*

>*A recent article...found that the highest estimates reported were for lifetime sexual assault among lesbian and bisexual women (85%)*

>*...bisexual women reported higher rates of non-penetrating sexual coercion and completed rape in adulthood than lesbian women*

>*...the risk of experiencing adult sexual assault is significantly associated with dating and pror sexual history, and the most common scenario involves persons who know each other*

### Sexual Minority Women and Gender Expression

>*One variable that may influence who is more likely to experience abuse is gender expression*

__Butch/Femme__: Masculine and feminine gender identities within lesbian and bisexual communities

>*An androgynous presentation has been found to be more similar to a butch aesthetic style, while women who do not identify with these terms have been found to present with a style more similar to a femme aesthetic*

>*...research...suggests gender expression is complex and comprises multiple factors including not only self-identity but also appearance, gender roles, and emotional expression*
>	__Appearance__: *encompasses dress, make-up, and hair style*

### Gender Expression and Trauma

>*Gender nonconformity has been associated being bullied, childhood harassment, and parental physical abuse among sexual minority men...gender nonconformity in adulthood among gay and bisexual men has been associated with paternal, maternal, and peer rejection in childhood*

>*More recently, a study with both sexual minority men and women in Finland indicated that recalled childhood gender nonconformity was correlated with negative ratings of parental relationships*

>*In a survey administered to US army soldiers, current masculinity among women was significantly associated with both childhood physical and emotional abuse, but childhood sexual abuse was associated with traditionally feminine current characteristics*

>*researchers have suggested that abuse is likely to impact one’s gender identity development*

>*It is thus possible that a girl exhibiting a masculine gender role is targeted for abuse because this nonconformity is not well tolerated or accepted, or that the abuse itself results in a more masculine gender role encompassing greater independence and self-reliance. For example, an abused child may attempt to act less characteristically female if being female was perceived as the risk for abuse.*

### Summary of Hypotheses and Tests
#### Hypotheses
1. *...women identifying as butch and scoring as more masculine will be more likely than femme women and those scoring as feminine to identify as lesbian//gay and less likely to identify as bisexual*
2. *...women identifying as lesbian/gay versus bisexual will not differ in reported rates of childhood abuse, but bisexual women will be more likely than lesbian/gay women to report adult sexual assault*
3. *...women identifying as butch, androgynous, and scoring as more masculine, especially on the gender role dimension of gender expression, will report more childhood abuse than women identifying as femme and scoring as more feminine*
4. *...women identifying as femme and scoring as more feminine, especially on appearance, will report more adult sexual assault than women identifying as butch, androgynous, and scoring as more masculine*
5. *...among lesbian/gay women only, those identifying as femme and scoring as more feminine (especially on appearance) will report more adult sexual assault than non-femme identified women and those scoring as more masculine*
## Methods
### Procedures

__Sampling Methods__:
- snowball
- targeted

>*...study fliers were sent electronically to over 200 LGB listservs, website groups, and organizations*

>*...given that bisexual women and LGB people of color are more difficult to recruit, targeted advertising was sent to venues specifically focused on these groups*

>*The present study received IRB approval from the University of Washington Human Subjects Division*

### Measures
>*...the survey included questions on socio-demographics, gender expression and identity, childhood trauma, and adult sexual assault*

### Socio-demographics
>*...we assessed participants' age; sexual orientation; race/ethnicity; education level; income; and city size*

### Gender Expression and Identity
>*Gender expression was assessed using the 15-item Gender Expression Measure among Sexual Minority Women*

GEM-SMW assesses:
- Appearance
- Gender Roles
- Emotional Expression

### Childhood Trauma
>*Childhood trauma was assessed using the Childhood Trauma Questionnaire*

Thee CTQ assesses five kinds of maltreatment on a 5-point scale (From 1/Never true to 5/Always True):
- Emotional Abuse
- Physical Abuse
- Sexual Abuse
- Emotional Neglect
- Physical Neglect

>*...scoring guidelines also allow four abuse classifications...: none or minimal, low to moderate, moderate to severe, and severe to extreme*
