## Formatting

- All lab reports should be submitted as .pdf files via the Blackboard Assignments page (to prevent formatting issues)
- Each section should have a header (Abstract, Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussions, References)
- Please use a font size of 11/12 points
- Generally, APA is a good format to follow (particularly for references)

## Components of the Lab Report

- __Title__: The title should be meaningful and informative as to the content of the report, using as few words as possible.
- __Name__
- __Affiliation__ (Boston University)
- __Abstract__: A brief summary (~200 words) of the paper. It should be concise, precise, and must cover four important points
	- What you did
	- How you did it (procedure)
	- What the results were (findings)
- __Introduction__: Your introduction should include the following:
	- A broad statement of the issues investigated
	- What previous observations have led to you undertaking this study
	- The purpose of your study
		- Research Question
		- Hypothesis
	- The significance of your predicted results (how would your findings advance the field?)
- __Materials & Methods__: Information in the Methods section should be sufficiently comprehensive to allow any reaser to be able to replicate your study.
