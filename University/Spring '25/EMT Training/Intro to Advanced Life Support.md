## Training
>Highest level of pre-hospital care

Training consists of 1,200-1,800 hours of training (about a year full-time, 2 years part-time)
- Didactic classroom: 700-1000 hours
- Clinical internship: 250-400 hours
- Field internship: 250-400 hours

## Scope of Practice
ALS providers are able to do procedures such as:
- IV/Catheter insertion
- Advanced airway
- EKG and cardiac monitoring and manual defibrilation
- CPAP
- Needle decompression
- Administer medications such as advanced cardiac, respiratory, and pain medications
