## Five Areas of NREMT
1. Airway Respiration and Ventilation
2. Cardiology and Resuscitation
3. Trauma
4. Medical/OBGYN
5. EMS Operations

The NREMT is 85% Adult and 15% Pediatrics

## 1-2-3-4 Method
1. What is it in simplest terms?
2. What are the risk factors for developing said emergency or disease?
3. What are the hallmark signs or symptoms that are going to appear when a patient has this emergency or disease?
4. How do you treat it?

### Vitals

__Normal blood pressure lies between__: 100-139 systolic over anything less than 100 mmHg

__Normal heart rate lies between__: 60-99 bpm

__Normal SPO2 lies above__: 94%

__Normal respiratory rate lies between__: 12-24 breaths per minute

__Normal temperature lies between__: 96.8 to 98.9 degreed Fahrenheit

__Normal blood glucose levels lie between__: 70-100 units
