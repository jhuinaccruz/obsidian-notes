__Combinatorics__: The art of counting

__Combination__: Combinatorics where order doesn't matter

__Permutation__: Combinatorics when order does matter

__Permutation Formula__: Used when there are `n` options and we want to pick `k` out of those in a certain specific order
$$
P(n, k) = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!}
$$

__Combination Formula__: Used when there are `n` options and we want to pick `k` out of those in no specific order
$$
C(n, k) = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!(k!)}
$$
