__Conditional Statements__: Whenever P is True, Q is also true, in other words
$$
{P}\implies{Q}\leftrightarrow \lnot{P}\lor{Q}
$$
- __Hypothesis__: P
- __Conclusion__: Q

__⇒__: Implies

__False Hypothesis__: When P is false, P -> Q remains true

>*When the statement on the left of the “ ⇒ ” does not hold, then we are not in a situation that is meant to be addressed by the claim, so we cannot assert that the claim is False. Therefore, the claim must be True*

__Truth Table__: where
- Columns are mathematical statements
- Rows are the assignments of truth values to the constituent statements
- The values within are the corresponding truth values of the mathematical statements

__Converse__: Inverting P and Q such that Q -> P

__Inverse__: Negating P and Q such that ¬P  -> Q

__Contrapositive__: Both conversing and inversing P and Q such that ¬Q -> ¬P

__<->__: Logical equivalence ("if and only if")
