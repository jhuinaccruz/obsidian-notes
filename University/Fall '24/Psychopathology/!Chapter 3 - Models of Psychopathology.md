One-dimensional Models
- refers to a paradigm, school, or conceptual approach that is believes in a *single* kind of cause 
- faulted in ignoring information from other available data/models
Multidimensional Integrative Models
- more dynamic, acknowledges that a disease can have *multiple* causes
- some models are:
	- [[Biological]]
	- [[Behavioral]]
	- [[Emotional]]
	- [[Social]] and Cultural
	- [[Developmental]]
	- [[Environmental]]

## Genetic Contributions

[[Chromosomes]]: determine mental and physical aspects
> Development and behavior is *almost always* __polygenetic__, *except* for single-gene determinants like Huntington's disease

Genetics account for less than 50% of psychopathological variations
> Life events account for more
