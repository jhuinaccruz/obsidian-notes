>Psychologists make a distinction between fear and anxiety.

__Anxiety__ and __fear__ have a negative impact on the body

__Anxiety__:
- results in *some somatic tension*
- is *future-oriented*
 > Anxiety is the feeling that one cannot predict or control upcoming event(s)
 
 __Fear__:
 - results in a *strong sympathetic system arousal*
 > Fear is an immediate alarm reaction characterized by strong escapist tendencies in response to present danger or life-threatening emergencies
 
__Both__ can result in a panic attack
