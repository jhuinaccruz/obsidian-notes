[[Psychopathology]]: study of psychological dysfunction
## Criteria for Evaluating Psychological Disorders:

- [[Deviance]]: outside expected behavior (social norms)
- [[Distress]]: anguish and turmoil experienced by the individual
- [[Dysfunction]]: impaired ability to fulfill daily life tasks
	- impairment/dysfunction needs to be pervasive and/or significant
- [[Danger]]: the infliction (or possibility) of harming oneself or other(s)

## Cultural Context

In order to understand and be open to people's differences, one must employ:
- Cultural competence: ability to understand cultural values across multiple cultures
- Cultural humility: ability to learn and integrate from other cultures whilst respecting your own and others

>[!info] Both are important in order to understand the contexts and backgrounds of people, to better serve and create harmony between two separate people
