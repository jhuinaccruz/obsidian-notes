## Mechanisms of [[Drug Tolerance]]
- __Pharmacokinetic__ (metabolic)
- __Pharmacodynamic__
- __Behavioral__
- __Acute__

## [[Behavioral Tolerance]]
> Heroin has a high potential for overdose that can be offset because of *repeated* and *increasing dosage* use, though in some cases there is a such thing of *sudden loss of tolerance*

Drugs can be classically conditioned with the *environment in which the drugs are consumed* becoming the *conditioned stimulus*
	__Compensatory Reaction__: makes up for the *expected effects* of the *expected consumption* of a drug, often the *opposite* effects of the drug
> __Drug__ + (After conditioning) __Environmental cues__ = __Physiological and/or Behavioral Compensatory Reaction__ + __Drug Effect__

## __Drug Withdrawal and Dependence__
- __Withdrawal__: negative symptoms resulting from chronic drug use is (abruptly) stopped
	- resulting from the body's *compensatory responses*
- __Dependence__: the physiological state of being *reliant* on a substance

## Drug Sensitization
__Sensitization__: the body increases some effects of a drug
- results in a leftward shift in a dose-response curve
