>*Psychoactive drugs are substances that alter a person's mental state or consciousness. They can affect mood, thought processes, perception, and behavior. These drugs interact with the brain's neurotransmitters, which are chemicals that transmit signals between nerve cells*

- Minor Stimulants
	caffeine
- Antipsychotics
	Thorazine
- Antidepressants
	Prozac
- Anxiolytic
	Valium
- Sedative-hypnotics
	alcohol
- Opiates
	morphine
- Hallucinogens
	LSD
