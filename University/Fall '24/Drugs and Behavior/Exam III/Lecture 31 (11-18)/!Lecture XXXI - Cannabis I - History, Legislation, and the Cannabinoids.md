## Terminology
__Tetrahydrocannabinol__ or __THC__: psychoactive compound
	not the only cannabinoid, over 100 including __cannabidiol__ (CBD)
	the psychoactive compounds are located in the resin of the trichome of the bud of the female plant
__[[Hemp]]__: a *cannabis plant* that contains 0.3 percent or less of THC per legal standards
__[[Cannabis]]__: a *cannabis plant* that contains more than 0.3 percent of THC per legal standards
	two different species:
	- *Cannabis sativa*
	- *Cannabis indica*
__[[Marijuana]]__

The hemp plant(s) are useful for rope, linen, and paper made from its stalks

## Uses

The leaves and buds of the plant is smoked

The psychoactive parts of the resin can be ingested as edibles

## History

__12,000 years ago__: cannabis believed to originate in central Asia, cultivated for its fiber in China and India
__2350 BCE__: mention a plant being used to make rope in Ancient Egyptian "Pyramid Texts", also used to treat glaucoma and inflammation
__Mid 1600s__: hemp became an important part of the economy in colonial America
	used for rope, cloth, canvas, and rigging for ships
__1800__: French troops brought back cannabis (hashish) from Egypt
__1850s__:added to the US Pharmacopoeia
__1930s__: the term "marihuana" was promoted by the U.S. government as a racist attempt to demonize its use by linking it to immigrants in Mexico, leading to swift criminalization of marijuana
__1937__: [[Marijuana Tax Act]]
__1942__: removed from the USP
__1964__: THC is first isolated in Israel
__1970__: scheduled into Schedule I as a part of the __[[Controlled Substances Act (1970)]]__
	the Marijuana Tax Act was repealed as well
__Mid-1970s__: more than a dozen states have decriminalized possession of marijuana

>Today, about 68% of adults in the U.S. support recreational cannabis legalization

>In August 2023, the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services issued a recommendaon to the DEA that cannabis be reclassified from Schedule 1 to Schedule 3 under the Controlled Substances Act

### Phytocannabinoids
__Delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol__ has received the most attention due to its well-known psychoactive, analgesic, anti-emetic, and appetite-stimulating effects
### Therapeutic Use
__[[Sativex]]__: whole plant medicinal cannabis extract
	developed to treat multiple sclerosis and neuropathic pain
__[[Epidiolex]]__: used to treat two rare forms of epilepsy ([[Lennox-Gastaut Syndrome]] and [[Dravet Syndrome]])
