## Defining Drug Addiction
__Drug addiction__ is abnormal (compared to "normal" drug use)
> __Drug addiction__ is hard to define

>[!quote]
>From drugabuse.gov
>*A chronic, relapsing disorder characterized by compulsive drug seeking, continued use despite harmful consequences, and long-lasting changes in the brain*

The [[DSM-5]] does not define addiction, instead recognized classes of drugs and specific diseases related to substance use
- __[[Substance-Induced Disorders]]__
- __[[Substance-Use Disorders]]__

## Progression of Substance Use
1. __Non-problematic/recreational use__
2. __Escalation of drug use__
3. __Compulsive/problematic use__
4. __Abstinence (Withdrawal)__
5. __Relapse__
