__Overt Attention__: Selecting information by moving the eyes
__Covert Attention__: Indirectly selecting information without moving eyes

__Feature Search__: Searching for a target with a unique feature that is not shared by surrounding distractors

__Conjunction Search__: A slow, serial visual search task requiring the identification of a target defined by a combination of two or more features

__Neuropsychology__: Treating or studying people with brain injury or neurological disease

__Hemispatial Neglect__; Brain damage on one side of the brain causes reduced awareness of the other side, most often from the right (parietal lobe) to the left
