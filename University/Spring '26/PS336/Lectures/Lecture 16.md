>*Memory integrates multiple sources of information to infer what happened in the past, which can lead to "memory illusions" similar to perceptual illusions*

__Are memories resistant to change?__: Two ways of studying
- Lab experiments
- Real life events (case study)

__Flashbulb Memory__: Vivid and long-lasting memories for unexpected, emotionally laden, and consequential events

__Downsides of Eyewitness Identification__:
- Misattribution of familiarity
- Effects of feedback
- Judgment of relative similarity

>*There is not good evidence that true memories get repressed and then recovered through therapy*

__Memory Recovery Therapy__: Now considered an unreliable and harmful pseudoscience
