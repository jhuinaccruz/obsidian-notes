__What promotes a strongly-encoded memory?__
- Recency
- Repetition
- Spacing
- Deep processing
- Organization (through narrative, for example)
- Imagery and elaboration

__Intersession Interval__ (ISI): The space between study sessions
- __Massed Practice__: When the ISI is 0

__Retention interval__ (RI): The space between the last study session and a test

__Rorher and Pashler (2007)__: A study of ideal ISI
- Two groups learned Swahili over the course of either 10 days or 6 months, having an ISI of either 1 day or one month respectively

__What promotes memory retrieval?__:
- Strong distinctive cues
- Similar or reinstated context

__Encoding Specificity__: The specific stimuli that were present when a memory was encoded

__Godden and Baddeley (1975)__: Divers were told to learn words either on land or underwater
- The biggest differences in score was not based on setting, but based on the words that matched the setting
- *Conclusion*: Items being learned that match a specific environment are learned and remembered easier
