__Extremism__: One of the more extreme forms of prejudice
- Rooted in the same psychological mechanisms as other prejudice

__Hate Crime__: A criminal act motivated by biases and targeting protected characteristics (defined by law)

__Hate Speech__: An offensive or prejudiced expression
- Not a criminal act (in most cases)

>*Anti-Asian hate crime rose 149% in 2020 in large urban areas across the US, spiking in March and April as Covid-19 cases increased*

>*Prejudice escalates and is reinforced by stereotypes, cultural narratives, and perceived threats*

__Hate Groups__: Organized groups targeting specific people
- Ideologically rooted in hostility or superiority
- People join said groups because
	- An identity search
	- Desire for meaning/purpose
	- Perceived threats or grievances
	- Identity uncertainty

>*Targeted messaging simplifies complex ideas; its framing(s) and medium(s) influence interpretations and shapes beliefs*
