>*Prejudice can be expressed at multiple levels: conscious beliefs, automatic evaluations, behavioral tendencies, and physiological reactions*

__Variable__: Characteristic(s) that vary from person to person or situation to situation

__Operational Definition__: Concrete representations of hypothetical constructs
- Directly observable by nature

__Common Methodologies__:
- Correlational
- Observational
- Experimental
- Meta-analysis: Derives a single and more powerful conclusion from multiple independent studies
	- The meta-analysis is only as good as the original studies ever were

__A valid measure of prejudice should have__:
- Construct Validity: Captures the construct we care about
- Convergent Validity: Relate to similar measures of prejudice that exists
- Discriminant Validity: Not strongly related to theoretically unrelated constructs
- Predictive Validity: Predict relevant outcomes

__Self-Report Measure__: The most common part of studying prejudice, where attitudes, opinions, motivations, and behaviors are self-assessed and reported as such
- Attitude Questionnaires: Rate the extent of agreement with statements about groups
- Advantages include efficiency, ease of administration, can cover multiple topics and doesn't require equipment
- Disadvantages include
	- Social Desirability Bias: Tendency to respond in socially desirable ways
		- Can conceal true attitudes and opinions
	- Memory Bias/Recall
	- Self-awareness

__Strategies for Self-Report Limitations__:
- Ensure anonymity
- Build in lie scales (two to three questions that capture the extent to which people lie)
- Measure social desirability
- Deception techniques (such as the Bogus Pipeline)
- Use indirect measures

__Indirect Measure__: Does not rely on participants explicit self-report through
- Advantages include reduction of the social desirability bias, reduced reactivity
- __Indirect Behavioral Measures__: Behavioral Measures that appear to have nothing to do with prejudice and discrimination
- __Indirect Physiological Measure__: Assess changes in body's responses to stimulus
	- Its main advantage is beyond voluntary control
- __Implicit Measurement Tools__: Reduce social desirability response bias
- __indirect Cognitive Task__:
	- Affective Priming: Priming through faces, then provide an ambiguous stimulus then judge stimulus as pleasant or unpleasant
		- People are more likely to rate ambiguous image consistent with stereotypes or evaluation associated with the prime
	- Affect Misattribution Procedure
	- Implicit Association Test
		- Weak predictor of individual behavior
	- __IAT__
		- Low test-retest reliability
		- Weak correlation between scores and actual behavior
