__Prejudice__: Occurs between groups; involves the evaluation and subsequently biased perception of a group based on real or imagined characteristics of the group

__Discrimination__: Treating people differently based on group membership

>*Discrimination can occur at multiple levels*

__Why don't the ABCs match up?__:
- Social norms
- Motivation to control prejudice
- Self-presentation concerns
- Situational constraints
- Automatic vs deliberative processes

__Explicit Attitude__: Deliberate, self-reported
__Implicit Attitude__: Automatic, often outside awareness
