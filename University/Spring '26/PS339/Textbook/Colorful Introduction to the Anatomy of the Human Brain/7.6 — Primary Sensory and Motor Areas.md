__Primary Visual Cortex__: Taking up much of the occipital lobes, located largely in the longitudinal fissure

__Primary Auditory Cortex__: Receives the most of the input from auditory relay nuclei of the thalamus; located in the superior temporal lobes largely in the lateral fissures

__Primary Gustatory Cortex__: Received most of their input from the gustatory relay nuclei of the thalamus; located on the inferior border of the parietal lobes largely in the lateral fissures

__Primary Motor Cortex__: Sends most of their motor fibers to motor circuits of the brain stem and the spinal cord; located in the precentral gyri

__Primary Somatosensory Cortex__: Receives most of their input from the somatosensory relay nuclei of the thalamus; located in the postcentral gyri

__Primary Olfactory Cortex__: Receives most of their input from the olfactory relay nuclei of the thalamus, located on the interior surface of the frontal lobes
