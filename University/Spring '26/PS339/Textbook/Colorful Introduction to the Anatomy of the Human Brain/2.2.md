__Anterior__ (Rostral): Frontmost
__Posterior__ (Caudal): Backmost

__Dorsal__: In a prone position, on top of the spinal cord
__Ventral__: In a prone position, towards the stomach/chest

__Medial__: Towards the midsagittal plane
__Lateral__: Away from the midsagittal plane

__Superior__: Towards the dorsal surface of the head
__Inferior__: Towards the ventral surface of the head
