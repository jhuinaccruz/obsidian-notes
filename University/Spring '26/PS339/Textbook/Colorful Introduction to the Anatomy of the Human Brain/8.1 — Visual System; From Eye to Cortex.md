__Retina__: A layered structure lining the back of each eyeball
- Contains five layers
	- The furthest layer from the pupil contains the visual receptors, and the closest contains the retinal ganglion cells

__Optic Nerves__: Bundles of retinal ganglion cell axons leaving each eyeball

__Optic Chiasm__: An X-shaped structure on the inferior surface of the hypothalamus, where the retinal ganglion cell axons decussate

__Optic Tracts__: Bilaterally projects from the optic chiasms to the lateral geniculate nuclei
- Composed of the axons from the retinal gangiion cells

__Lateral Geniculate Nuclei__: Relays visual information from the optic tracts to the corresponding primary visual cortex via optic radiations

__Optic Radiations__: Neural pathways from the Lateral Geniculate Nucleus to the corresponding primary visual cortex
