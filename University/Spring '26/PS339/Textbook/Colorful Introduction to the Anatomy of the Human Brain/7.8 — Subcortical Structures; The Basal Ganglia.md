__Basal Ganglia__: A pair of bilateral cerebral nuclei that play a role in movement control
- Composed of the
	- Caudate
	- Putamen
	- Globus Pallidus

__Amygdala__: A structure of both the limbic and basal ganglia

__Caudate Nucleus__: Coming out of each amygdala in the anterior temporal lobe
- *Caudate means tail-like*

__Putamen Nucleus__: Located in each hemisphere just lateral to the globus pallidus, connected to the anterior end of the caudate by a series of fiber bridges

__Striatum__: Caudate + Putamen
- *Striatum means striped structure*

__Globus Pallidus__: Located bilaterally between the thalamus and the putamen
