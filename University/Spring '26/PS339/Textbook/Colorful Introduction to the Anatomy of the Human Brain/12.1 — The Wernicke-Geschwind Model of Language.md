__Wernicke_Geschwind Model__: Attributes the various processes of language to specific areas in the left cortex, since right cortical lesions rarely disrup language
- Considered lncorrect in several areas; provided a framework for the study and treatment of aphasia
- There are seven areas that mediate language-related activities
	- Primary auditory cortex (hearing the word)
	- Primary Visual Cortex (seeing the written word)
	- Mouth/Thrroat area of the Primary Motor Cortex (mediates the motor responses of speech)
	- Wernicke's Area mediates comprehension of spoken language
	- Angular Gyrus: Translates the image of the word into auditory code to be passed onto Wernicke's Area
	- Broca's Area: Stores programs of speech pduction and activates the adjacent motor cortex to produce speech
	- Arcuate Fasciculus: Connects Wernicke's to Broca's Area, such that the former's comprehension functions activates speech programs in the latter

__Wernicke's Area__: Located in the superior temporal cortex of the left hemisphere; posterior to the primary auditory cortex
- The center of language comprehension

__Angular Gyrus__: Located on the boundary between the parietal and temporal lobe
- Translates images of written words into auditory code

__Broca's Area__: The area of the left frontal cortex; anterior to the mouth region of the primary motor cortex:
- Contains the motor programs for speech

__Arcuate Fasciculus__: White matter tract connecting Wernicke's Area with Broca's Area
