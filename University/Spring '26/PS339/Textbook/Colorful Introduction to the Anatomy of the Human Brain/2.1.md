__Horizontal Sectioning__: Slices of the brain cut parallel to the horizon (assuming the subject is in an upright position)
__Sagittal__: Parallel to the vertical plane that divides the brain into its left and right
- __MIdsagittal__: A cut from the very center of the brain

__Coronal__ (Frontal): Parallel to the surface of the face

__Cross Section__: Slices cut at right angles to the long axis of any long and narrow structure
