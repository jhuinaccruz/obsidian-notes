__Telencephalon__: The cerebral hemispheres, one of the two divisions of the forebrain

__Diencephalon___: The region of the brain between the telencephalon and the mesencephalon, one of the other two divisions of the forebrain

__Mesencephalon__: The midbrain; the region of the brain stem between the diencephalon and the metencephalon

__Metencephalon__: The region of the brain stem between the mesencephalon and the mylencephalon; one of the two divisions of the hindbrain
- Contains the cerebellum and the pons

__Myelencephalon__: The most posterior region of the brain, between the metencephalon and the spinal cord
