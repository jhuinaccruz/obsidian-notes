__Ventral Pathway of Recognition__: Occipital into Temporal Lobe 
- __Fusiform Gyrus__: Specializes in high-level recognition
- **Parahippocampal Area:** Often associated with the **Parahippocampal Place Area (PPA)**, which processes "scenes" or landscapes (e.g., recognizing a kitchen vs. a forest). 
- **Lateral Occipital and Posterior Inferior Temporal Cortex:** These regions handle the perception of object form and structure before they are "labeled" with meaning.
- **Anterior Inferior Temporal Cortex:** Where visual information is integrated with memory and semantic knowledge.

__Ventral and Dorsal Visual Cortical Stream of Recognition__: Starts from V1
- Dorsal: Into Superior Longitudinal Fasciculus (SLF-I) into the Posterior Parietal Cortex
- Ventral: Into the Inferior Longitudinal Fasciculus (SLF-III) into the Inferior Temporal Cortex

__Collateral Sulcus__: Divides the Fusiform Gyrus (Inferior Lateral) from the Parahippocampal and Lingual Gyrus

__Lingual Gyrus__: Processes early shape and color

__Dorsal Pathway of Attention__: Posterior Parietal Cortex + Superior Frontal Cortex
- Includes help from the Superior Colliculus (overt attention)

__Ventral Pathway of Attention__: Temporoparietal junction into the Ventral Prefrontal Cortex
- Includes the Pulvinar (covert attention)

__Supramarginal Gyrus__: At the end of the Lateral Fissure

__Angular Gyrus__: Posterior to the Supramarginal Gyrus

__Primary Motor Cortex__: Brodman's Area 4

__Parietal Reach Region__: Brodman's Area 5/7

__Supplementary Motor Area__: Dorsal Brodman's Area 6

__Premotor Cortex__: Ventral Brodman's Area 6

__Parietal Eye Field__: Brodman's Area 7

__Frontal Eye Field__: Brodman's Area 8

__Broca's Area__: Includes motor aspects of speech; Brodman's Area 44/45
