__Phineas Gage__: Damage to his left orbito-frontal cortex and medial prefrontal cortex changes him
- No longer observes social conventions
- Behaves unethically and irresponsibly

__Lesion__: A localized structural change

__Broca's Area__: Influences the ability to speak properly

__Wernicke's Area__: Influences the ability to comprehend language and speech

__Penfield's Homunculi__: The distorted representation of the somatosensory cortex

__Phrenology__: A pseudoscience that claimed that the brain is an aggregate of many suborgans with a specific psychological faculty
- Other less sound claims include that
	- Said facility's productivity is proportional to size of organ
	- The brain could be mapped one-to-one between brain centers and mental functions
	- Mental facilities are completely innate
	- Suborgan size could be measured from the outside based on the bumps on the skull
	- Character traits are specific mental facilities within the brain

__Brain Specialization__ (according to current cognitive neuroscience): There exist networks/systems of several interconnected brain units, each piece performing specialized processing while also contributing to other specialized behaviors.
- In other words, the mind is a result of the concerted operation of multiple physical brain systems

__Neocortex__: Composed of six highly-structured layers that makes up about 90 percent of the cerebral cortex

__Mesocortex__: Made up of six layers including the paralimbic cortex cingulate, parahippocampal, insular, and orbitofrontal cortices

__Allocortex__: About 1-4 layers including the hippocampal complex and primary olfactory cortex

__Gyrus__: A "bulge" part of the a fold in the brain; outward
__Sulcus__: A "groove" part of a fold; inward

__Fissure__: Similar to a sulcus, except significantly deeper

__Brain's Gross Anatomy__: Consists of four lobes
- Frontal
- Parietal
- Occipital
- Temporal

__Cytoarchitectonics__: The local architecture of neural cell connections by which Brodmann cortical area subdivisions of the brain is possible (52 in total)

__Stria of Gennari__: A ribbon of highly stained cells in the calcarine sulcus of the occipital cortex
- Area 17 ends and 18 begins at the end of the Stria

__Saggital__: From the side
__Coronal__: From the front/back
__Horizontal__: Top-down view
