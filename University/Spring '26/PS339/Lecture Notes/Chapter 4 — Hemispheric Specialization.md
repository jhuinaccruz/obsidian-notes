>*Each hemisphere has its own private sensations, perceptions, thoughts, and ideas all of which are cut off from the corresponding experiences in the opposite hemisphere...in many respects each hemisphere appears to have a separate 'mind of its own'*

>*Individuals with different cognitive strategies/strengths don*t necessarily imply hemispheric differences*

__Language has a strong _ bias__: Left Hemisphere

__Vision has a modest _ bias__: Right hemisphere

__Corpus Callosum__: A group of 200-300 million myelinated axons

__Homotopic__: Going from one hemisphere to the equivalent structure in the other hemisphere
__Heterotopic__: Going from one hemisphere to a non-equivalent structure in the other hemisphere

__Split-Brain Patients__:
- Behavioral differences after require careful testing
- The "non-dominant" hemisphere still processes information

__96% of _ are left-hemisphere dominant for language, whereas 60% of _ are__: Right hand, left hand

__WADA Test__: Anesthetize one hemisphere (amytal into the carotid artery), test, and repeat with other hemisphere

__Hemispatial Neglect__: Neglect of the left side of space from right hemisphere damage to the parietal and frontal lobes

>*Hemispheric asymmetries are subtle*
