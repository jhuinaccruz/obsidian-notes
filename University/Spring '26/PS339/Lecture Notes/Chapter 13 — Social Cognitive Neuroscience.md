__Default Brain Network__: A series of connections between brain areas that show higher activation when the rest of the brain is at rest
- Many areas activated by social cognition overlap with many regions in the default mode network
	- Medial Prefrontal Cortex
	- Posterior Cingulate Cortex
	- Retrosplenial Cortex
	- Temporo-Parietal Junction/Angular Gyrus
	- Anteior Superior Temporal Sulcus
- Activity in the Dorsal Medial Prefrontal Cortex, for example, increases curing daydreams and decreased during goal-directed behavior

__Theory of Mind__: The way we attribute mental states to ourselves and others, and how we understand different mental states from our own
- In other words, our cognitive capacity to understand the mental states of others

__Sally-Anne Task__: A false belief task designed to understand whether somebody understands that others theory of mind differs with reality
- Participants see "Sally" leave a marble in a basket then leaves the room. "Anne" then comes and moves the marble to a box. Participants then get asked where "Sally" will look for the marble first
	- *Passing* assumes that the participant says that the marble will be searched first in the basket, *failing* assumes the marble will be searched in the box

__Mindblindness__: An inability to understand social goals, intents, or beliefs of others

__Mentalization__: Thinking about the minds of others and one's own mind
- It is a broader ability to perceive and interpret human behavior in terms of mental states
- Can be seen as a form of imaginative mental activity

>*Autism can be seen as a failure to develop mentalization abilities

__Phineas Gage__: Suffered damage to his left orbitofrontal cortex and medial prefrontal cortex and survived
- His personality completely changed; he no longer observed social conventions
	- His impaired social cognition was perceived as a result of the specific brain lesion

>*Social cognition is seen a product of network effects rather than a localized process*

>*In VLSM studies, there is an intersection between socially inappropriate behavior and orbitofrontal cortex lesions*

>*Orbitofrontal Damage also results in reduced self-perception in real time, but not when played back on a video. In other words, they self-judge as inappropriate and such even though in the moment the same self-judgement was missing*

__Self-Referential Processing__: The way we think about things in relation to ourselves
- Self-referential processing leads to stronger memory encoding due to an increased depth of processing
- Primarily anatomically located in the medial prefrontal cortex
	- Decreased activity in self-referential learning, but less so in relation to oneself

__implicit Bias__: An unconscious attribution of stereotype to a specific group of people
- Can be tested with an IAT; logic similar to a Stroop test
	- Measures reaction times between tested subgroups
	- Amygdala fMRIs show that implicit bias score is proportional to amygdala activity
