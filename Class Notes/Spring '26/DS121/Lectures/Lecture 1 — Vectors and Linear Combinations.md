__Vectors__: A container for multiple different values with the ability to add together multiple vectors as well as multiplication by a scalar
- Addition: Adding two vectors
$$
\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 6 \end{bmatrix}
$$
- Multiplication by scalars
$$
2 \times \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}
$$

>*You cannot multiply vectors by one another or add a constant to a vector*

__Vector Space__: A set of objects that can utilize vector operations

__Column Vector__: A list of real numbers with only one column
__Row Vector__: The transpose of a column vector

__Euclidean Space__: The set of all vectors with `n` entries such that it is of dimension `n`, denoted by `R^d`
