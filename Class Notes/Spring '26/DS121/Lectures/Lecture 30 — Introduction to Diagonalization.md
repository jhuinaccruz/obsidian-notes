__Diagonalization__ (Eigendecomposition): A method of factorization of matrix $A$ denoted as
$$
A = PDP^-1
$$
- $P$ and $D$ are square matrices of the same dimensions as $A$
	- $P$ is invertible
	- $D$ is a diagonal matrix (non-zero entries only on the diagonal)
- $A$ is diagonizable if it is similar to a diagonal matrix
- In general, $\forall k ≥ 1, A^k = PD^kP^{-1}$
- __The Diagonalization Theorem__: A square matrix $A: n\times n$ is diagonizable if and only if $A$ has $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors
	- The $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors become the columns of $P$, and the diagonal entries of $D$ become the eigenvalues
