>*The eigenvalues of a triangular matrix are on its main diagonal*

__Algebraic Multiplicity__: The number of times a certain eigenvalue appears among all the other eigenvalues

__Geometric Multiplicity__: The number of linearly independent eigenvectors
- In other words, the number of unique eigenvectors

__Similarity__: For two square matrices $A$ and $B$, similarity occurs when there exists another matrix $P$ such that $$P^{-1}AP = B \iff A = PBP^-1$$
- __Similarity Transformation__: Changing $A$ into $B$
	- Similarity implies they have the same characteristic polynomial and the same eigenvalues
