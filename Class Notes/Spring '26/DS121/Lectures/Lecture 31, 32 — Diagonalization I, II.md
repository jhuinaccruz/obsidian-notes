>*The geometric multiplicity of an eigenvalue is less than or equal to its algebraic multiplicity*

__Symmetric Matrices__: Square matrices such that their transpose is equal to itself
- Any two given eigenvectors from different eigenspaces are orthogonal

__Orthogonally Diagonizable Matrix__: A square matrix that can be expressed as the product of an orthogonal matrix $P$ and diagonal matrix $D$
$$
A = PDP^\top = PDP^{-1}
$$
- __Spectral Theorem__: A square matrix is orthogonally diagonizable if and only if it is a symmetric matrix
