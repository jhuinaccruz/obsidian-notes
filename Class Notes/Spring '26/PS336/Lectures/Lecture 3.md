__Cortex__: Outer sheet of grey matter, containing neurons

__White matter__: Contains the extension of axons

__Gyrus__: Ridge within the cortex (deeper grooves)
__Sulcus__: Fold within the cortex (wrinkles)

>*Pattern of gyri and sulci vary from person to person, but the general pattern persists amongst brains*

__Anterior__ (Rostral): Towards the front
__Posterior__ (Caudal): Towards the back

__Superior__ (Dorsal): Towards the top of the head
__Inferior__ (Ventral): Towards the feet

__Lateral__: Away from the midline
__Medial__: Towards the midline

__Lobes of the brain__:
- Frontal
- Parietal
- Occipital
- Temporal

>*The cerebellum is not a part of the cerebral cortex*

__Sources of human brain activity data__: Sometimes through invasive electrical recordings (if clinically indicated). but some non-invasive procedures includes
- Electroencephalography (EEG): Aggregate electrical activity is recorded through electrodes on the scalp
- Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging (fMRI): Localized changes in blood flow is recorded over time
	- Measures blood flow as an indirect index of aggregate neural activity in voxels (about 2-3 mm per)
