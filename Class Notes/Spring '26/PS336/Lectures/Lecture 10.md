__Object-Based Attention__: Prioritizes entire perceptual objects rather than just specific spatial locations

__Attentional Blink__: The second of two rapid visual targets is missed when it appears 200-500ms after the first

__Same-Object Advantage__: Observers process and detect targets faster and more accurately when they appear on the same object rather than on two different object

__Rapid Serial Visual Presentation__: Displays images one at a time in rapid succession at a single fixed location, enabling fast processing speeds

>*Visual search requires short term memory*

__Sternberg Memory Scanning Paradigm__: A recognition task that is easy to perform accurately

__Serial Exhaustive Scanning__: Relating to the memory paradigm

>*Failure to notice changes can reflect limitation either in attention or memory*

__Flicker Paradigm__: Two similar images are displayed in rapid alternation separated by a blank screen

__Iconic Memory__: High-capacity, short duration visual sensory memory that holds an exact image of a scene
