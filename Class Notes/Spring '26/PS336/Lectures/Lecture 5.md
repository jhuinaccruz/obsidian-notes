>*Visual inputs only provide partial information about what's really out there, since the fovea is the only one that has high acuity and the eye shifts frequently*

Cues for perceiving depth and distance include:
- __Occlusion__: Nearer objects block the view of farther objects
- __Motion Parallax__: Moving one's head changes the retinal position of objects that are farther away more
- __Binocular Disparity__: Each eye has slightly different perspective (more so for near objects)
- __Lens Adjustment__: Muscles in the eyes do different amounts of work to focus on objects farther away versus closer ones

__Likelihood Principle__: Some interpretations of an input is more probable than others

>*Perception entails unconscious inference*

__Bistable Image__: Can be seen as one of two interpretations (each is contradictory to each other)

__Where do perceptual prior beliefs come from?__:
- Knowledge of regularities in the environment
	- Evolutionary experience
	- Individual experience, learning and plasticity

__Bayesian Inference__: A formal process for combining prior expectations with perceptual evidence
$$
\text{Prior probability}\ \times \text{Likelihood of the evidence} \rightarrow \text{Posterior Probabiltiy}
$$
