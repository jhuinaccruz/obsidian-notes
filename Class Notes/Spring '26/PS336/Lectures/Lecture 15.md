- Recency
- Repetition
- Spacing
- Deep processing
- Organization/Narrative
- Imagery and elaboration
- Testing
  Strong Distinctive Retrieval Cues

>*One thing that doesn't seem to help: tailoring instruction to an individual's learning style. Instead, it's generally beneficial to teach everyone with multiple formats*

>*...certain types of content might be better suited to one format than another*

__Deese__ (1959): Recall is better if the items were semantically related
- An important caveat to note is that participants also often falsely recalled non-presented items related to the list

__Roediger and McDermott__ (1995): Support Deese's finding of false memory reports

__DRM LIst__: A false memory paradigm task where participants are presented with lists of semantically-related words at encoding and then asked to recognize said words after a delay

>*Declarative memory retrieval is an inference about what occurred in the past*

>*Memory involves reconstructing, not merely reproducing past events*

>*Similar constructive processes underlie both accurate and inaccurate memories*
