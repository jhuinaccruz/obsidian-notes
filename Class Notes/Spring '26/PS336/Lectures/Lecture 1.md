__Why study mental activity?__: Three main ideas
- Introspection
- Behaviorism
- Neural reductionism (all psychology just reduces to pure neural activity)

## Introspection and Structuralism

__Analytic Introspection__: Analyzing conscious experience through verbalizing its sensation components
- Issues arose with this method of psychological studying due to external validity and sensations being consciously accessible

__Behaviorists describe behavior as__: A function of stimulus history, placing emphasis on experiments in nonhuman animals

__Behaviorism__: The main focus is to generalize human learning and behaviors
- Criticisms include that it can't really explain language acquisition
