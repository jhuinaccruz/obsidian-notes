__Memory at multiple levels of description__;
- Computational: The goal to be able to respond differently now is based on what occurred previously
- Algorithmic: Serial Exhaustive Search, iconic, Short-Term Memory, Signal Detection Theory
- Implementational: The medial temporal lobes are critical role for certain memory processes

__Patient H.M.__ (1953): Underwent an experimental surgery for seizure disorder
- A resection of medial temporal lobes bilaterally, including the hippocampus and adjacent structures was performed
- He suffered anterograde amnesia as a result of the procedure
- His language, general intelligence, and short-term memory remained intact
- Long-term, episodic, encoding, and one-shot memory is presumably hippocampus-dependent

__Anterograde Amnesia__: Trouble learning new information
__Retrograde Amnesia__: Trouble remembering old information

__Mirror Tracing__: A task involving drawing with a mirror's reflection as the only visual feedback
- Retrains the brain's lateral structures to communicate

__Word-Stem Completion__: A task involving priming the subject to certain words by mere exposure, then giving the first couple of letters, with the idea that the subject completes the word

__Double Dissociation__: Comparing two patients with different lesions, such that one has the opposite symptoms of the other

__Potential Causes of Amnesic Symptoms__:
- Viral infection
- Hypoxia
- Korsakoff Syndrome: Thiamine deficiency causes damage to structures that mediates connections with the hippocampus
