__Unilateral__: On one side of the body
__Bilateral__: On both sides of the body

__Ipsilateral__: When talking about neural signals, signals that start and end on the same side
__Contralateral__: When talking about neural signals, signals that start and end on different sides

__Decussate__: When contralateral fibers cross over from one side of the body to another
- __Decussation__: The point at which they cross over
