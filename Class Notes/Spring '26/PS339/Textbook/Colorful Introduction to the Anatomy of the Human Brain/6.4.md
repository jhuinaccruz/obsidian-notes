__Thalamus__: One of the two major structures of the diencephalon; a two-lobed structure that sits on top of the brain stem
- __Massa Intermedia__: A thalamic nucleus that in the center of the third ventricle, also known as a cerebral comissure because of its midline position

__Relay Nuclei__: Part of the thalamus; they receive sensory information from receptors and relay it to the appropriate areas of the cerebral cortex
- Known nuclei include:
	- Lateral geniculate nuclei: Relay visual information
	- Medial geniculate nuclei: Relay auditory information
	- Ventral geniculate nuclei: Relay tactual information

__Internal Lamina__: The layers of white matter in each love of the thalamus, appearing as white Y-shapes from a superior perspective of each thalamus lobe
