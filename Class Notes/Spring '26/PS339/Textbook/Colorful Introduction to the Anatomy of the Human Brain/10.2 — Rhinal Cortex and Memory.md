__Rhinal Cortex__: Composed of two areas around the rhinal fissure; Entorhinal and Perirhinal Cortex.
- Largely focused on olfactory processes, but also recently discovered it has an important role in object-recognition memory

__Rhinal Fissure__: Located in the medial temporal lobe

__Entorrhinal Cortex__: An area of the medial temporal cortex that extends from the subicular cortex to the perrhinal cortex

__Perrhinal Cortex__: The cortex of the Rhinal Fissure
- Circles around the Rhinal Fissure
