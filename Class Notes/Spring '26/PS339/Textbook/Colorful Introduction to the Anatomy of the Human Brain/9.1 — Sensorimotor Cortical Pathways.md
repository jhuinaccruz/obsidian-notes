>*Before an accurate movement can be initiated, the sensorimotor system must know the current position of the body parts that are to be moved...supplied to the posterior cortex by the visual, auditory, and somatosensory systems*

__Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex__: An association cortex that provides a mental representation of objects to which the subject is going to respond and initiate voluntary movement
- Receives information from the sensorimotor cortex
- Sends projections to four areas of the secondary motor cortex
	- Supplementary Motor Area
	- Premotor Cortex
	- Two Cingulate Cortex

__Secondary Motor Cortex__: Primarily outputs to the primary motor cortex

__Supplementary Motor Area__: Located in the dorsal frontal cortex; anterior to the precentral gyrus; laregly located in the longitudinal fissure
- Also receives input from the somatosensory cortex

__Premotor Cortex__: Located in the ventral frontal cortex; anterior to the precentral gyrus; largely located in the lateral fissure

__Cingulate Motor Area__: Located in the cingulate gyrus
