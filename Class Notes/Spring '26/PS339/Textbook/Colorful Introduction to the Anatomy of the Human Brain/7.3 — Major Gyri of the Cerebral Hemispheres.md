__Gyri of the Frontal Lobe__:
- __Precentral Gyrus__: Anterior to the central fissure
- __Superior Frontal Gyrus__: Runs horizontally along the top of the lobe
- __Middle Frontal Gyrus__: Located between the superior and inferior frontal gyri
- __Inferior Frontal Gyrus__: Located just inferior to the middle frontal gyrus

__Gyri of the Temporal Lobe__:
- __Superior Temporal Gyrus__: Just below the lateral fissure
- __Middle Temporal Gyrus__: Between the superior and temporal gyrus
- __Inferior Temporal Gyrus__: Just inferior to the middle temporal gyrus

__Gyri of the Parietal Lobe__:
- __Postcentral Gyrus__: Just posterior to the central fissure
- __Angular Gyrus__: Located at the border between the parietal, occipital, and temporal lobes
