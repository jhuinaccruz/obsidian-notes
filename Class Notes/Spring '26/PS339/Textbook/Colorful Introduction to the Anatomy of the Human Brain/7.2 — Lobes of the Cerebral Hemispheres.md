>*Each human cerebral hemisphere is divided by the central fissure and lateral fissure into four lobes*

__Frontal Lobe__: Anterior to the central fissures and anterior/superior to the lateral fissure

__Parietal Lobe__: At the top of each hemisphere, posterior to the central fissures and superior to the lateral fissures

__Temporal Lobe__: At the bottom of each hemisphere, inferior to the lateral fissures

__Occipital Lobe__: At the most posterior of each hemisphere, posterior to both the parietal and temporal lobes

>*The cerebral hemispheres are not functional units*
