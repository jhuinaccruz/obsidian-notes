__H. M.__: A medial temporal lobectomy resulted in mild retrograde amnesia and severe anterograde amnesia
- More specifically, his new long-term explicit memories were impaired
	- His short-term memories, as well as implicit memories, were intact
- His dissociation study suggests that the temporal lobes play an important role in converting short-term memories into long-term memories

__Hippocampal Formation__ (Hippocampal Complex): Composed of the hippocampus, dentate gyrus. and subicular cortex
- Originally blamed for H. M.'s condition

__Hippocampus__: Part of the allocortex; a large fold in the medial temporal lobe allocortex
- *Hippocampus means seahorse*

__Dentate Gyrus__: Part of the allocortex: A C-shaped structure that curves around the edge of the hippocampus

__Subicular Cortex__ (Subiculum): The transitional area between the hippocampus and neocortex

__Fimbria__: A bundle of axons along the medial surface of the hippocampus, carrying signals from the hippocampus to the fornix
