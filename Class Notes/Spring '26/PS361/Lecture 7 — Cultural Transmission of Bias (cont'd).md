__Representation__: Presence or absence of a specific group in media is communicative and reflects culture and meaning

>*A study done between 2014-2015 shows that men appear more in film and TV, and women are more sexualized in said media than men*

>*The same study found that sexual minorities are nearly invisible in major media roles*

>*Media portrays group in patterned roles, where role repetition shapes expectations of said groups*

__Media amplifies bias by__:
- Shaping visibility through categorization
- Assigning value and roles through evaluation
- Framing causality and frequency through attribution

>*Repetition and familiarity which in turn leads to normativity*
