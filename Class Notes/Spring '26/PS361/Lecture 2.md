__Privilege__: Unearned access to resources only readily available to some due to advantaged social group membership(s)

>*Privilege = power*

__According to Peggy McIntosh__:
- *White privilege* is "an invisible weightless knapsack of unearned assets and advantages"
- "There is silence and denial surrounding privilege"

__Invisibility Thesis__: Argues that white people regard themselves as racial actors
