__Happier moods can lead to \_, whereas more sad moods lead to \___: Superficial processing (more heuristics which can lead to stereotypes), systematic and detail-oriented

>*Specific emotions don't just make us "feel bad"; they can shape how we perceive and stereotype outgroups*

__Anger__: Most strongly associated to outgroup prejudice and stereotypes involving hostility and agression

__Disgust__: Linked to stereotypes of physical or moral impunity

__Anxiety__: Associated with stereotypes of unpredictability or incompetence

>*Angry people stereotype as much as happy people*

>*Emotion effects may depend on the specific emotion and target group*

__Emotion and Target Interaction__: Different emotions can facilitate the stereotyping of certain groups but not others
- Disgust facilitates gay men stereotyping
- Anger facilitates stereotyping African American and Arab men (rather than gay men)

__Moderators of Emotion-Based Stereotyping__:
- Egalitarian values
- Counter-stereotypic thoughts

>*When egalitarian values are salient stereotyping can be reduced*

__Sensitivity__: Differences in the "experience" of emotions affects the tendency to stereotype
