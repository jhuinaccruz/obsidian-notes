__Ingroup Favoritism__: Favoring ingroup members over outgroup ones

>*The minimal group paradigm: What is the minimal condition for group bias?*

__Outgroup Homogeneity Effect__: The underestimation of outgroup variability

>*Ingroup members seen as unique individuals*

__In choosing subgroups. category salience depends on__: Social context and the nature of the interaction

__Self-Categorization Theory__: People define themselves as members of social categories, then intergroup contexts make certain identities salient.
- In contexts where we take on the group personality, we depersonalize and become part of the group

>*Ambiguous individual are more likely to be classified as the outgroup, more prevalent in individuals with higher ethnocentrism*

__Top-Down Processing__: Pre-existing knowledge guides perception
- Can occur alongside (but also restrict the ability of) bottom-up processing
- Relies on
	- Stereotypes/beliefs
	- Individual difference

>*Stereotypes can bias categorization when certain category combinations become salient and thus biased towards (especially under ambiguous conditions)*

>*High-prejudice individuals may rely more heavily on stereotypes to reduce ambiguity when category membership is unclear*

>*Situational cues can activate top-down processing, since observers tend to focus on the category that makes someone "different" in a given context*

>*Stereotyping is a natural process reflecting kernels of truth*
