>*Race is a social construction; not rooted in biology or genetics but rather created and maintained by social systems that have been shaped by historical, political, and economic forces*

__Race__: A construct based on physical characteristics
- Often perceived on physical characteristics
- Tied to power and history

__Ethnicity__: Linked to culture, shared traditions, and identity (including aspects of language, nationality and religion)

>*Both race and ethnicity are used to categorize people and carry social meaning and consequences*

>*Categories are not neutral; they carry meaning, value, and shape how people are treated*

>*Categories mean something because they structure access to resources and opportunity. They are the foundation for prejudice and discrimination, shaping patterns of inequality across societies*

>*Prejudice has moved away from overt and direct into something more subtle and more deniable and indirect due to changes in legal and social norms; equality is a widely-accepted value*

__Modern Prejudice__: Considered to be more subtle, indirect, and difficult to detect or prove

__Doleac and Stein__ (2013): Experimenters sold an iPod on Craigslist, changing the color of the seller's hand across various postings
- An additional posting of a Black hand with a tattoo was also used
- *Results*: The non-White posting received less bids, as well as lower offers
	- The tattoo hand received the worst outcomes
- *Conclusion*: Subconscious biases can affect even a "free" market
	- Biases can compound onto themselves
