__Categorization__: To combat information overload, we simplify things and put them into boxes of similar things

__Social Categorization__: Grouping people based on shared features, attributes, or functions
- Its benefits includes
	- Reducing cognitive complexity in social environments
	- Frees up mental resources for other tasks
	- Helps anticipate others' behavior
	- Guides future interactions
	However, its costs includes
	- Easier activation of stereotypes
	- Overgeneralization from groups to individuals
	- Biased judgments and decisions

__Primitive Categories__:
- Race
- Age
- Gender

>*These categories are primitive because they are immediate and visible as well as cognitively efficient, which in turn creates strong influences*

__Category Subtypes__: Combines information from multiple categories to provide additional social information
- Can be also based on traits or social roles

>*Stereotypes can be accurate, inaccurate, oversimplified, or overapplied*
