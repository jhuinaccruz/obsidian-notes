Two main theories:
- __Modern (Symbolic) Racism__: Racism is expressed indirectly as a framing of fairness, values, or personal responsibility
	- Lacks an acknowledge of social factors, past racism, and generalizes the outgroup
	- Inequality is interpreted as a result of individual choices
		- Seen as due to a belief in hard work and individual responsibility
	- People who uphold modern racism view themselves as fair and reasonable
- __Aversive Racism__: Racism is rooted in discomfort and biases rather than a value system
	- People express an interest for equality with an affective reaction that expresses (more often than not) subtle discomfort
	- Manifests itself in interaction discomfort, avoidance, and explicitly-less-engaged behavior
	- Bias can occur in ambiguous situations

>*Modern symbolic prejudice predicts political views to be more ethnocentric*

>*These theories show that people can maintain a non-prejudiced self image while also subtly expressing prejudice*
