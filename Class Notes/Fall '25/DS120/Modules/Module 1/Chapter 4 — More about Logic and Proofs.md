__Necessary Condition__: When Q is not enough to know if P is true, then ¬P -> ¬Q, or Q -> P

__Sufficient Condition__: When Q is enough to know that P is true, then Q -> P

__In an "Implies" statement, the hypothesis is the \_ condition, and the conclusion is the \_ condition__: Sufficient, Necessary

## Proving Logical Equivalences
__Associative Law__: Moving around parenthesis will not change results

__Distributive Law__: Conjunctives distribute amongst themselves, such that
$$
{P}\land({Q}\lor{R}) \leftrightarrow ({P}\land{Q})\lor(P\land{R})
$$
and:
$$
{P}\lor({Q}\land{R}) \leftrightarrow ({P}\lor{Q})\land(P\lor{R})
$$

__De Morgan's Laws__: Named after Augustus De Morgan, using negations to prove logical negations,
$$
\lnot({P}\land{Q}) \leftrightarrow \lnot{P}\lor{\lnot{Q}}
$$
and:
$$
\lnot({P}\lor{Q}) \leftrightarrow \lnot{P}\land{\lnot{Q}}
$$
