__Sentential Logic__: A formal system of logic where that uses sentence letters and logical connectives to determine a truth value

__Connective__: Used to connect logical formulas
- __And__: Conjunction (∧)
- __Or__: Disjunction (∨)
- __Not__: Negation (¬)

__Conditional__: If-then

__Biconditional__: If and only if, then...

>*In general, the negation of a universal (i.e. “ ∀ ”) claim is one of existence (i.e. “∃”), and vice versa.*
