__Confidence Interval__ (CI): The range in which the true mean of the population lies with a certain probability
- *95% confidence interval*: Using sample data from an infinite series, 95% of such intervals contains the true mean
$$
CI = {\text{sample mean} ± \text{margin of error}}
$$

__Level of Confidence__: Denoted by the `z` value

__Margin of Error__:  Denoted by
$$
\text{Margin of Error} = z \times \frac{\text{population standard deviation}}{\sqrt{n}} = x \times \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}
$$

__Population Mean__: To determine population mean
- The sample must be randomly selected
- The variable of interest must have a normal distribution in the population
- The population `µ` is unknown, but the standard deviation must be known

__Bessel's Correction__: The unbiased estimate of the population standard deviation, denoted by
$$
S = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{1}{(n-1)}(X_i - \bar{X})^2}
$$
where
- `S` denoted the estimation
- `(n-1)` is the degrees of freedom
