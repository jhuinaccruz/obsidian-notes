__Expectation__ (Expected Value/Mean): When dealing with a random variable, the average value  weighed by the probability that it comes up, calculated by the weighed average of the outcomes
$$
E[X] = \mu = \sum_{X = x} xPr(x)
$$

__Linearity of Expectation__: The expectation of a random variable which itself is a sum of two random variable can be described as the sum of their individual expectations
$$
E[X + Y] = E[X] + E[Y]
$$
