## The Therapeutic Index and Drug Safety

ED50 - median effective dose that is effective in 50% of subjects tested

TD50 - median toxic dose in 50% of subjects tested

LD50 - median lethal dose in 50% of subjects texted

[[Therapeutic Index]] - LD50 / ED50
	calculates drug safety
	*lower Index is less safe*
## Types of Drug Interactions

Antagonism: one drug diminishes the effect of another drug
	Shifts the dose response curve to the right, making a drug less potent
Additive: One drug increased the effect of another drug
	Shifts the dose response curve to the left, making a drug more potent
Potentiation/Synergism: Combining drugs increases the potency beyond simple additivity
	Shifts the dose response curve to the left, making it more potent
## Pharmacokinetics

[[Pharmacokinetics]]: how a drug is absorbed and processed through the body
- Absorption: how a drug gets into the blood
	injection
	inhalation
	diffused through membtanes
	oral (mouth)
- Distribution: where it goes in the body
- Metabolism: how the body alters and breaks it down
- Excretion: how the drug leaves the body

The effects of a drug depend on how quickly and efficiently it can pass through cell membranes
- most psychoactive drugs are lipophillic/hydrophobic (charged/non-polar)

Substances must enter and leave the bloodstrea by passing through capillaries by diffusion
	Lipid-soluble drugs diffuse easily through capillary walls

Bioavailability: the extent to which a substance becomes available to its intended destination in the body

[[Routes of Absorbing Substances]] - how and where a drug is administered determines how quickly it is available in the body
