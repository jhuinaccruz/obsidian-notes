Half life: index measuring the time of the process from absorption to elimination
	length of time for the blood concentration of a drug to be reduced by half
	Most drugs have an exponential (first-order) excretion curve, except alcohol ()

## First-order (exponential) kinetics of drug clearance
A constant fraction (50%) of free drug in the blood is removed during each half-life
	occurs because surplus enzymes are always avallable, so the rate is __concentration-dependent__
Most drugs are eliminated after about six half-lives, but the length of said half lives change considerably.

## Zero-order (constant rate) kinetics of alcohol clearance
- Alcohol is an exception: it is cleared from the body at a constant rate regardless of concentation
	occurs because the enzymes are saturated, and thus only work at a consistent rate
