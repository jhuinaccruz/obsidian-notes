>[!abstract] Learning Objectives
>- Understand the effects of opioids on physiology, cognitive performance, mood, euphoric “rush” and reinforcement
>- Learn about the harmful effects of continued opioid use, opioid use disorder, dependence & withdrawal
>- Understand some pharmacotherapies for OUD and how they work
>	- We’ll focus on Methadone, Buprenorphine & Suboxone treatment


The __*Big Three*__ effects of opioid drugs on neuron activity are:
- __Analgesia__
- __Euphoria__
- __Respiratory Depression__
- 
>CNS effects are primarily euphoria and analgesia

>Reinforcing effects are due to enhanced dopamine release in the nAc

Additional physiological effects include
- __Nausea__ and __vomiting__
- __Constipation__
- __Profuse sweating__
- __Pinpoint pupils__
- __Sleep/sedation__

Opioids affect __cognitive performance__ and __mood__:
- Opioid use is associated with inattention, difficulty concentrating, perceptual distortion, memory deficit and executive dysfunction
- Can also lead to a momentary intense euphoric feeling,

*High doses* of opioids can lead to comatose-like state with severe depression of breathing that can lead to death

>Opioid withdrawal is not as dangerous as alcohol or barbiturate withdrawal

Withdrawal symptoms begin about __6-12__ hours from the last dose
- Restlessness and agitation
- Yawning
- Chills and sweating
- Goosebumps
- Stomach, back and leg cramps
- Vomiting and diarrhea
- Twitching of extremities

## Pharmacotherapies
- *Short-term* detoxification ([[naltrexone]])
	- considered unpleasant, not recommended, considered dangerous
- *Long-term* interventions include
	- __[[Methadone]]__ (Methadone-assisted treatment)
		fully synthetic opioid and mu-opioid receptor agonist (high affinity and strongly-activating)
		must be administered at a clinic
		Stops other opioids from binding to the receptor
		Less addictive than heroin
		taken orally as a pill or liquid
		Long half-life (lasts all day)
		Reduces withdrawal and cravings for other opioids
			Additionally reduces exposure to dirty needles/blood-borne diseases
		People may become dependent on methadone, sometimes for life
	- __[[Buprenorphine]]__
		semisynthetic and partial mu-opioid agonist
		binds strongly but has a weak effect
		prescribed and taken home
		high potential misuse when crushed to take via IV/snuff
			sublingual forms such as [[Suboxone]] (buprenorphine + [[Naloxone]]) were created to offset such misuse

More on Suboxone:
- if taken __sublingually__: the antagonist (Naloxone) is not absorbed as well, buprenorphine is well absorbed
- If taken __otherwise__: the antagonist is absorbed and reduces the effects of buprenorphine
