### [[Schizophrenia]]
__Schizophrenia__ comes from Greek *schizen* "to split" and *phren* "mind"

>In "[[Psychotic Disorders]]" such as schizophrenia, people experience psychoses: disturbances in thoughts and perceptions that result in a loss of touch with reality

Treating schizophrenia consists of a combination of behavioral therapy and use of antipsychotic medications

### Symptoms

__Positive symptoms__ include:
- Hallucinations
- Delusions

__Negative symptoms__ include:
- Difficulty expressing emotions
- Difficulties for planning
- Lack of motivation
- Isolating oneself
- Disinterest in daily life

__Cognitive symptoms__ include difficulty with attention and/or applying information to make decisions

>The spectrum of schizophrenia is a heterogenous set of conditions that vary widely from person to person in severity, duration and presentation of symptoms

see also [[DSM-5]]

### 
>Schizophrenia is a chronic and debilitating disease affecting 0.5-1% of the U.S. population, which is 3X the prevalence of [[Multiple Sclerosis]]

>The age of onset of Schizophrenia is typically in early adulthood (~20.5 years old) but varies based on biological sex
