## Drug Scheduling

Controlled substances are regulated by the [[Controlled Substances Act (1970)]]
	divides them into five categories ("schedules")
Drug control policies have more than quadrupled the national prison population since the 1980s
	Despite this, millions of Americans continue to use illicit drugs
---
## Drug Use in the United States

- Fatal drug overdoses are the number one cause of accidental deaths in the United States (as of 2023)
---
## The [[Opioid]] Epidemic

- 1990s: first wave of opioid overdose deaths
	- overprescribed, pushed by marketing schemes
- 2010s: second wave of opioid overdose deaths
	- often mixed with heroin
- 2013 - present: 3rd wave of opioid overdose deaths
	- often laced with synthetic opioids (ex. fentanyl)

- the fourth wave: psychomotor stimulants with and without fentanyl
