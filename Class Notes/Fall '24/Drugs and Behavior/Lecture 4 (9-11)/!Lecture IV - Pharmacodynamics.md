## [[Pharmacodynamics]]

We use [[Dose Response Curve]]s to measure the effect of a drug on the body
- x-axis: range of doses (independent)
	typically in relation to body weight
- y-axis: physiological/behavioral effect of the drug (dependent)
	
ED50: median effective dose - the concentration at which 50% of the maximal effect of the drug is observed

Potency: refers to the relative strength of the drug
	measured at the ED50
Efficacy: refers to the ability to produce the *maximal* desired effect
- measured at the top of the dose-response curve(100%)
