*Behavioral Pharmacology*: first book published on the study of behavioral changes due to drugs

## Classes of Behaviors (for non-human animal models)
- #### Unconditioned
	Unlearned
- #### Conditioned
	making an association with an outcome
	includes two types:
	- ##### Classical
	- ##### Operant

## Unconditioned Behavioral Tests
- __Open Field Test__: measures spontaneous motor activity and anxiety
- __Hot Plate Test__: measures analgesia
- __Elevated Plus Maze__: measures anxiety
- __Incline Plane Test__: measures muscle tone (catalepsy)

## Drug Reward Reinforcement
- __Conditioned Place Preference (CPP)__
- __Intravenous Drug Self-Administration (IVSA)__

> A drug is *rewarding* if the animal will press the lever for infusions of a drug *or* prefer to spend time in a chamber where the drug was previously administered

## Conditioned Place Preference (CPP)
A form of [[Classical Conditioning]]
	This test relies on a conditioned association between drug effect and environment

If the drug is *rewarding*, the animal spends much more time in the environment associated with the drug.

If the drug is *aversive*, the animal prefers the environment not associated with the drug.

The test is divided in three phases:
- Habituation
- Conditioning
- Preference Testing
