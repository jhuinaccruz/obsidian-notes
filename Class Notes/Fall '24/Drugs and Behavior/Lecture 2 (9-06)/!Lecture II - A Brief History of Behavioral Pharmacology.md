[[Substance Use Disorder]] - mental health condition that affects the brain and behavior, such that it leads to an inability to control the use of said substance(s)

Drug -  a non-nutritional substance that alters physiology and/or behavior

[[Psychoactive Drug]] - compound(s) that affect thoughts, feelings, perception, behavior and level of consciousness
