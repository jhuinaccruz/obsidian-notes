## Cocaine
> Cocaine is a euphoria-producing stimulant drug with strong misuse/addictive potential

It is mostly produced in South America
- Occurs in remote jungle labs
	Colombia produces ~90% of the cocaine powder reaching the United States

### Origins
- Dates back to __2500 BCE__
	became sacred to the Incas
- __1786__: identified as *Erythroxylum coca* (by Lamarck)
- __1860__: isolated by German chemist Albert Neimann
- __Late 1800s__: used as a local anasthetic
- __Freud__ was an early enthusiast of cocaine use
	__1884__: Uber Coca
	>The psychic effect…consists of exhilaration and lasting euphoria, which does not differ in any way from the normal euphoria of a healthy person. One senses an increase of self-control and feels more vigorous and more capable of work. Opinion is unanimous that the euphoria induced…is not followed by any feeling of lassitude or other state of depression…It seems probable…that coca, if used protractedly but in moderation, is not detrimental to the body– that a first dose or even repeated doses…produce no compulsive desire to use it further; on the contrary, one feels a certain unmotivated aversion to the substance.
	
- __1914__: Harrison Narcotics Tax Act
	in response to the China opium problem
	> imposed taxed on the sale, distribution, manufacturing, importation, and distribution of coca leaves, opium, or any derived products
	- marked a shift in cultural perception from "a drug for intellectuals"

### Medical Uses

Cocaine can act as a local anesthetic
>When applied at high concentrations (e.g. topically or local injection), cocaine inhibits voltage-gated Na+ channels in axons, blocking action potential conduction

[[Procaine]] and [[Lidocaine]] are local anesthetics that were originally developed from cocaine

### Administration
#### Absorption
Cocaine has a base of 8.6 (weak)
- coca leaves are mixed with calcium carbonate to make it more alkaline

### Routes
- Insufflation (cocaine HCI)
- IV (dissolved cocaine HCI)
- Smoked
	degrades rapidly whe heated
	- crack is cocaine HCI mixed with sodium bicarbonate that is dried into "rocks"

>Free base is cocaine HCI + ammonia and then extracted with ether

## Synthetic Psychostimulants

### [[Amphetamine]]
Two isomers
- d (dextroamphetamine)
- l (levoamphetamine)
	both are most potent and addictive by injection or inhalation

### [[Methamphetamine]] (aka double-methylated methamphetamine)
More potent, faster-acting, lipid-soluble, chemically stable, and better blood brain barrier penetration than amphetamines
- crystalized form can be smoked, innjected and snorted

### Administration

#### Absorption
Amphetamines has a base of ~9 (weak)
	it is absorbed poorly by the digestive system, which in turn creates a more sustained level of amphetamine

### Distribution, Metabolism, and Excretion

__Both__ readily cross the blood brain barrier, although methamphetamine is more lipid-soluble than cocaine

> Both are metabolized in the liver, although up to ~40% of amphetamine may be excreted unaltered

Excretion depends of the urine
- __Amphetamines__ have a half life of 16-34 hours at a basic pH or as little as 7 hours with an acidic pH
- __Cocaine__ has a half life of an hour
- __Cathinone__ has a half life of about 90 minutes
