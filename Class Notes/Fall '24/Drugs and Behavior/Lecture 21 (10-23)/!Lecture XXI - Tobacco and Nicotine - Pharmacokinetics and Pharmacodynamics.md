>Cigarette smoking is on the decline in the U.S., but vaping is mostly still on the rise in teens

Vaping is *likely safer* than cigarettes
- vaping does not deliver carcinogens that are products of combustion (e.g. carbon monoxide)
- on the contrary, vaping contains vitamin E acetate and diacetyl, which are poorly regulated by the Food and Drug Administration
	- vaping-associated lung injury (E-cigarette or Vaping Product Lung-related Injury)

Nicotine raises heart rate and blood pressure, and vaping can cause nicotine poisoning

## Routes of Administration
### Preparation
- __snuff__: sniffed into nose
- __moist__: between lip and gum
- __swallowed juice__: chewing tobacco/snuff
- __inhalation__: lung inhalation
	- flue cured: cigarettes (acidic)
	- air-cured: cigar/pipe (basic smoke)
	works fast, quick bioavailability

## Pharmacokinetics
>Nicotine is a *weak* base

>In its ionized state, such as in acidic environments of the stomach, nicotine does not rapidly cross membranes, producing low absorption

>At basic pHs, there is little ionization and thus more “free base,” which is lipid soluble and readily crosses membranes (e.g. basic cigar/pipe smoke via buccal membranes of the mouth)

>Acidic (ionized) smoke (e.g. from cigarettes) can be produced, and can only be absorbed through inhalation in the lungs, which are so efficient at gas exchange that pH has no real effect on absorption

Vaping one "dose" produces nicotine blood levels similar to one cigarette

CYP2A6 - allele linked with being less likely to become dependable smokers

Biological females clear nicotine faster than biological males
	probably due to CYP2A6 being induced by estrogens

Urine affects excretion based on its pH
	Acidic pH allows excretion, basic pH allows reabsorption

## Pharmacology

The primary addictive component in tobacco is nicotine

Nicotine acts by binding to __nicotinic acetylcholine receptors__ (nAchR) located in the central and peripheral nervous systems
- a __competitive nAchR agonist__ that activates an influx of cations into the neuron (sodium, potassium, and calcium)
- nAchR is located *post-synaptically* where they act to depolarize neurons when they are stimulated by acetylcholine or nicotine
	- also located *presynaptically* on axon terminals where they act as neuromodulators that stimlate the release of [[acetylcholine]] or other neurotransmitters such as [[glutamate]]
- nAchR receptors consist of 5 subunits
	alpha4 and beta2 are important for tobacco reinforcement
