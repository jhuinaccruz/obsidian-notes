>[!quote]
>*The causative form in Japanese (使役形, **しえきけい**) expresses causation, meaning that someone **makes** or **lets** someone else do something. It's similar to "make someone do" or "let someone do" in English. Here's an overview of how to form and use it:*

>[!quote]
>From Genki II:
>*In this [lesson](Lesson%2022.md), we learn yet another verb derivation called the “causative form” When you use the causative form of a verb, you can describe who makes someone do something, and who lets someone do something.*

## Conjugations
| verb   | causative form |
| ------ | -------------- |
| う-verb | 〜あせる           |
| る-verb | 〜させる           |
| 来る     | こさせる           |
| する     | させる
