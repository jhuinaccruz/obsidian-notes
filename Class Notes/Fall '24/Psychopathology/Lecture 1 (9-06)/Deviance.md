>*Deviance in psychology refers to behaviors that deviate from societal norms or expectations. These behaviors can range from minor infractions to serious crimes. While sociology often focuses on the societal factors that contribute to deviance, psychology delves deeper into the individual factors that may influence such behaviors.*

## Examples of Deviant Behavior

- Substance abuse
- Crime
- Anorexia nervosa
- Self-harm
- Gambling
