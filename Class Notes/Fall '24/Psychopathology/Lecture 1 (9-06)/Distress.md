>*Distress is a psychological state characterized by negative emotions, physical symptoms, and cognitive impairments. It can be a significant factor in deviant behavior.*

## Examples of Distress

- **Substance abuse:** Individuals may turn to drugs or alcohol to cope with distress.
- **Self-harm:** Cutting, burning, or other forms of self-injury can be used as a way to manage emotional pain.
- **Crime:** Distress can lead to impulsive or violent behavior, such as theft or assault.
- **Eating disorders:** Anorexia nervosa or bulimia nervosa can be a way to cope with emotional distress.

- *some* distress is normal in *some* situations
