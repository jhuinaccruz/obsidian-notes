>[!quote]
>According to Gemini:
>
>*Neuroimaging is a collection of techniques used to visualize and study the structure and function of the brain. These methods provide valuable insights into various neurological and psychiatric disorders.*

Methods of neuroimaging can be divided into two separate kinds:
- Structural
	- [[CAT]] scan (Computer Axial Tomography)
	- [[MRI]] (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)
- Functional
	- [[PET]] (Positron Emission Tomography)
	- [[SPECT]](Single-Photon Emission Computed Tomography)
	- [[fMRI]] (functional MRI)
	- [[BOLD]] (Blood Oxygenation Level Dependent)
