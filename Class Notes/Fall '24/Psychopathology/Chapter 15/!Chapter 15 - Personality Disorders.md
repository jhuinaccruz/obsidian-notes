## Overview
Personality Disorder(s) are enduring, inflexible predispositions
- They are maladaptive, causing distress and/or impairment in functioning
- *High* comorbidity with other disorders
- Generally show poor prognosis
- Additionally, patients dont feel that treatments are necessary

__Egosyntonic__: behaviors, values and feelings that are consistent with one's self image
__Egodystonic__: thoughts, impulses, behaviors and feelings that are alien, distressing, and inconsistent with one's own self-concept, personality, and values

## [[Five-Factor Model of Personality]]
Also known as the "Big Five":
- __Openness__ to experiences
- __Conscientiousness__
- __Extraversion__
- __Agreeableness__
- __Neuroticism__

>Cross-cultural research establishes the relatively universal nature of the five dimensions

## Statistics
- About 10% of the general population has some sort of personality disorder
- They are thought to begin in childhood
	- Tend to run a chronic course if untreated and/or transition into a different personality disorder
>[!info]
>Men more often show traits like aggression and detachment; women more often show submission and insecurity

- Men are more likely to be antisocial
- Men and women are equally likely to be histrionic
- Comorbidity is the rule, not the exception

## [[Personality Disorder Clusters]]
### [[Cluster A: Paranoid, Schizoid, and Schizotypal Personality Disorders]] (Odd or Eccentric)
- __[[Paranoid Personality Disorder]]__
	>A pervasive pattern of social and interpersonal deficits marked by acute discomfort with reduced capability for close relationships, as well as by cognitive or perceptual distortions and eccentricities of behavior
- __[[Schizoid Personality Disorder]]__
	>A pervasive distrust and suspiciousness of others such that their motives are interpreted as malevolent
- __[[Schizotypal Personality DIsorder]]__
	>A pervasive pattern of detachment from social relationships and a restricted range of expression of emotions in interpersonal settings
### [[Cluster B: Antisocial, Borderline, Histrionic, and Narcissistic Personality Disorders]] (Dramatic or Erratic)
- __[[Antisocial Personality Disorder]]__
	People suffering from this disorder repeatedly and chronically violate the rights of others through manipulative, impulsive, deceitful, and impulsive behavior
	- Often show early history of behavioral problems such as [[Conduct Disorder]] ("callous-unemotional" is a type of such disorder that can evolve into ASPD)
	- Some theories related to the causal factors of ASPD include:
		- __Neurobiological__ factors such as:
			- Underarousal of the cortex
			- Cortical immaturity
			- Fearlessness
			- [[Gray's Model of Reward]]
		- __Early developmental problems__ that may affect later development of ASPD based on one's familial structure (e.g. families with inconsistent discipline and support, and histories of criminal and violent behavior)
		- __Genetic__ predispositions
		[[Sociopathy]] and [[Psychopathy]] are common terms that refer to very similar traits
	>Recent research suggests that psychopathy is a less reliable predictor of criminality
	- More focus is placed on preventative measures and rehabilitation, however incarceration is more often than not the only viable option.
- __[[Borderline Personality Disorder]]__
	People with this disorder suffer from unstable moods and self-image, which propagates itself into various aspects of their life.
	- Additionally, the affected report feelings of impulsivity and fear of abandonment
	- Can manifest itself externally into unstable relationships, suicidal gestures and self-harm
	- Comorbidity rates are high with other mental disorders (specifically mood disorders, substance use disorders, and eating disorders)
	- Factors that can cause BPD include:
		- __Genetics__
		- __High Emotional Reactivity__ (may also be genetic)
		- __Impaired function of [[Limbic System]]__
		- __Early Trauma and Abuse__
	- __[[Triple Vulnerability Model]]__:
		- __Generalized Biological Vulnerability__
		- __Generalized Psychological Vulnerability__
		- __Specific Psychological Vulnerability__
	- Treatment can include:
		- __[[Antidepressant Medication]]__ (only provide some short-term relief)
		- [[Dialectical Behavior Therapy]] shows the most promise as a treatment
