## Programming Bigger Programs

__Precision__: The proportion of examples labeled "yes" that are really "yes" in a spam classifier

__Recall__: The proportion of true examples that were found

__F-measure__: The combination of precision and recall by finding the harmonic mean of the two

$$
\frac{2}{\frac{1}{r} + \frac{1}{p}} = \frac{2}{\frac{r + p}{rp}} = \frac{2rp}{r+p}
$$
where:
- __r__ = recall
- __p__ = precision
