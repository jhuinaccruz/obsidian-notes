__Behavior is motivated because__:
- Behavior is variable and can change even if the same history of reinforcement
- Behavior is persistent and keeps going despite periods of non-reinforcement

## Behavior is Goal-Oriented

>*Animals do not just learn rigid associations between a stimulus and a response: Behavior is inherently flexible and a means to an end*

## Behavior is Motivated By Reinforcers
>*Learning and performance are separable constructs*

>*Reinforcement is not necessary for learning, although it is necessary to motivate the animal to translate what it has learned into performance*

## Incentive Learning

__Incentive Learning Theory__: For a motivational state to energize a behavior, the animal must first learn that the action leads to a particular reinforcer and that this reinforcer has a positive effect on the motivational state

>*Hunger does not blindly energize all behaviors — rather it selects a specific set of behaviors that will lead to the goal of ending hunger.*

>*The effect of a motivational state on behavior depends on what has been learned about the reinforcer while in the motivational state*

## Anticipation
>*A great deal of motivated behavior seems to anticipate, rather than respond to, need*

>*An evolutionarily well adapted animal will not wait until it is in a state of need to begin seeking reinforcers, like food or water.*

>*Animals must (and do) perform behaviors to respond to depletion — but these should be saved for emergencies rather than the everyday state of affairs* 

## Paradoxical Reward Effects
__Contrast__: The effect of reward on performance is partly determined by past history of reinforcement
- __Positive__: Experience with a smaller reward makes a larger reward feel especially large
- __Negative__: Experience with a larger reward makes a smaller reward feel especially small

__Frustration__: When an animal expects a large reward and gets either a small reward or no reward, it often becomes frustrated
- Frustration is proportional to the difference between the expected and received reward

>*Frustration is unpleasant, evokes withdrawal, and the rat will learn to escape conditioned stimuli that elicit it*

__Punishment by Reward__: Occurs if non-rewarded performance is rewarded

__Partial Reinforcement Extinction Effect__ (PREE): Partially reinforced behavior is more persistent than continuously reinforced behavior
- There is more to learning than associative strength or habit
- PREE provides a way to help make behavior more persistent
