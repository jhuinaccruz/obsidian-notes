## Oxygen

__Oxygen__: Provides a higher concentration of oxygen to alveoli in the form of a gas

__Indications for oxygen___:
- Shortness of breath
- Signs of hypoxia
- Altered mental status
- Pain caused by ischemia

__Contraindications for oxygen__: None

__Adverse effects__: Dry mouth and nausea

__Considerations__: No open flames nearby, always give oxygen to patients with respiratory distress, evaluate need in COPD patients

## Metered Dose Inhalers

__Metered Dose Inhalers__: A bronchodilator in the form of a powder/mist

__Indications for MDI__:
- Treats bronchospasm with reversible obstructive airway disease
- Shortness of breath
- Current prescription for rescuer inhaler type MDI

__Contraindications for MDI__:
- Hypersensitivity
- Unconscious
- Unable to physically use device
- Maximum dose reached prior to arrival
- Device not prescribed to patient
- Inhaler is a maintenance inhaler

__Adverse effects__:
- Anxiety
- Tachycardia
- Muscle tremors
- Coughing

__MDI increases the effects of other__: Nervous system stimulants

## Albuterol (Nebulizer)

__Albuterol__: A bronchodilator in the form of a mist inhaled via nebulizer

__Indications for Albuterol__:
- Treatment of bronchospasm with reversible obstructive airway disease
- Signs and symptoms of shortness of breath
- Current prescription for rescuer inhaler type MDI

__Contraindications for Albuterol__:
- Hypersensitivity
- Unconscious
- Unable to physically use device
- Maximum dose achieved prior to arrival

__Dosage for albuterol__:
- 2.5mg in 3mL saline for adults
- 1.25mg in 3mL saline for pediatric patients
- Administer over 5-15 minutes depending on flow rate

__Adverse Effects of Albuterol__:
- Restlessness
- Tremors
- Dizziness
- Palpations
- Tachycardia
- Peripheral vasodilation
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Hyperglycemia
- increased blood pressure
- Paradoxical bronchospasm

__Additional Considerations for Albuterol__:
- Oxygen tank or air compressor is required
- Flow rate should be 6-8 lpm
## Ipratroprium Bromide

__Ipratroprium Bromide__: A bronchodilator in the form of a mist that is inhaled via nebulizer

__Indications for Ipratroprium Bromide__:
- Treatment of bronchospasm with reversible obstructive airway disease
- Signs and symptoms of shortness of breath
- Current prescription for rescuer inhaler type MDI

__Contraindication for Ipratroprium Bromide__:
- Hypersensitivity
- Unconscious
- Unable to physically use device
- Maximum dose reached prior to arrival
- Bromide hypersensitivity

__Dosage for IB__:
- Adults: 500mcg in 3mL saline
- Pediatrics: 250 mcg in 3mL saline
- Administer over 5 to 15 minutes depending on flow rate

__Adverse effects of IB__:
- Restlessness
- Tachycardia
- Anxiety
- Hypertension
- Chest pain of cardiac origin

__Additional considerations for IB__:
- Oxygen tank or air compressor required
- Flow rate should be 6-8 lpm
- (For pregnancy) Should be given only if the potential benefit justifies the risk to the fetus
- May precipitate arrhythmia

## Acetylsalicylic Acid

__Acetylsalyciic Acid__ (ASA): A platelet inhibitor, analgesic, antipyretic, and anti-inflammatory agent in the form of a tablet that is consumed orally and/or chewed

__Indications for ASA__: Acute chest pain indicative of cardiac problem

__Contraindication for ASA__:
- Hypersensitivity
- Unconscious
- Unable to swallow
- Allergic to aspirin
- Evidence of a recent or severe internal bleed
- Bleeding disorder

__Dosage for ASA__: 162mg to 325mg

__Adverse effects of IB__:
- Heartburn
- GI bleeding
- Stomach pain
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Wheezing in allergic patients
- Prolonged bleeding

__Additional considerations for ASA__:
- Do not administer for pain from trauma or fevers in children
- Caution with patients taking an anticoagulant
## Nitroglycerin

__Nitroglycerin__: A vasodilator in the form of a tablet or spray taken sublingually

__Indication for NTG__: Acute chest pain indicative of cardiac problems

__Contraindications for NTG__:
- Hypersensitivity
- Systolic BP <100mmHg
- intracranial bleeding or head injury
- Patient already met max dose
- Erectile dysfunction medications within last 24 hours

__Dosage for NTG__: A tablet or spray, with a max dose of three doses five minutes apart

__Adverse effects of NTG__:
- Headache
- Tingling under tongue
- Hypotension
- Syncope
- Flushing
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Diaphoresis
- Muscle twitching
## Glucose

__Glucose__: Raises blood sugar in the form of a gel or tablet taken orally

__Indications for Glucose__;
- Change of mental status
- History of diabetes
- Blood glucose level known to be less than 80 mg/dL

__Contraindications for Glucose__:
- Unconscious
- Unable to swallow
- Suspected stroke
- Nausea
- Vomiting

__Dosage for Glucose__:
- Adults: 15g or dose until patient regains mental status (Maximum dose of two tubes, five minutes apart)
- Pediatric (<20kg): 7.5mg (half a tube)

__Adverse effects of Glucose__;
- Hyperglycemia
- Nausea
- Vomiting

__Considerations for Glucose__: Patient must have a patent airway and be able to follow commands

## Epinephrine

__Epinephrine__: A bronchodilator and vasoconstrictor in the form of a liquid administered intramuscularly

__Indications for Epinephrine:
- Signs of severe allergic reactions causing airway breathing and/or circulatory compromise
- Anaphylactic reaction

__Contraindications for Epinephrine__: None when used in life threatening emergencies that increases the effects of other nervous system stimulants

__Dosage for Epinephrine__:
- Adults: 0.3mg, with a max dose of two, five minutes apart
- Pediatric: 0.15, with a max dose of one

__Adverse effects of Epinephrine__:
- Hypertension
- Tachycardia
- Headache
- Burning at injection site
- Anxiety
- Sweating
- Pale skin
- Headache
- Palpations
- Dizziness
- Syncope (in asthmatic children)
## Diphenhydramine

__Diphenhydramine__ (Benadryl): An antihistamine in the form of a tablet that is taken orally

__Indications for Benadryl__: A mild allergic reaction

__Contraindications for Benadryl__:
- Hypersensitivity
- Asthma
- Glaucoma
- Pregnancy
- Hypertension
- Infants
- Monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAO) use
- Alcohol use

__Dosage for Benadryl__: 25-50mg

__Adverse effects for Benadryl__:
- Sleepiness
- Dry mouth
- Dry throat
- (situationally) Simulation/hyperactivity

__Administration considerations__:
- Can use in severe allergic reaction after administration of epinepherine
- Pediatric dose is weight based

## Acetaminophen

__Acetaminophen (Tylenol)__: An antipyretic and analgesic in the form of a tablet that is taken orally

__Indications for Tylenol__:
- Relief of mild pain or fever
- Headache
- Muscle aches

__Contraindications for Tylenol__:
- Hypersensitivity
- Liver failure

__Dosage for Tylenol__: 500-1000mg

__Adverse effects for Tylenol__: Allergic reaction

__Additional considerations for Tylenol__:
- Take caution for overdosing with other OTC medications with acetaminophen in them
- Pediatric dose is weight-based
- Take caution with liver disease

## Ibuprofen

__Ibuprofen__: A non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) in the form of a tablet taken orally

__Indications for Ibuprofen__:
- Relief of mild pain or fever
- Headache
- Muscle aches

__Contraindications for Ibuprofen__:
- Hypersensitivity
- Trauma other than isolated extremity trauma
- Known GI bleeding

__Dosage for Ibuprofen__: 200-400mg

__Adverse effects of Ibuprofen__:
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Stomach pain
- Bleeding
- Allergic reaction

__Considerations for ibuprofen__:
- Do not give with aspirin
- Do not give for pain caused by trauma
- Pediatric dose is weight based
