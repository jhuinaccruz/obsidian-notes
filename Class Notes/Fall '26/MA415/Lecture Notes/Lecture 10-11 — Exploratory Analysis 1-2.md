__Pythagorean Expectation__: Predicts winning percentage $$W_p = \frac{R^2}{R^2 + RA^2} = \frac{1}{1 + (RA/R)^2}$$
- where $R$ is runs scored and $RA$ is runs allowed

__Jeffreys' Rule__: Uses a beta distribution to determine confidence intervals
