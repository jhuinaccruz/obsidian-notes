__Relational Data__: Tables with relations between them, denoted through keys that uniquely identify an observation
- __Relation__: Formed between two tables through *joining* primary and foreign keys
	- __Inner Join__: Matches pairs of observations with equal keys
	- __Outer Join__: Keeps observations that appear in at least one table
	- __Semi Join__: keeps observations that match
- __Primary Key__: Uniquely identifies an observation in its own table
- __Foreign Key__: Uniquely identifies an observation in another table

>*One-to-many relationships are based on duplicate keys*
