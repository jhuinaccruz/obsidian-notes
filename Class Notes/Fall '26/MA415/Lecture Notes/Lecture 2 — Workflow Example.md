__Log-Log Model__: A method of regression where you take the natural logarithm of both predictor and response values before applying a linear regression line
$$
Y \propto X^\beta \implies \log(Y) = \alpha + \beta \log(X)
$$
- where:
	- $Y$: Response value(s)
	- $X$: Predictor value
	- $\alpha$: Constant
	- $\beta$: Coefficient of $X$
